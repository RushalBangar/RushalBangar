## 🌟 About Me

I am **Rushal Bangar**, a B.Tech student specializing in **Artificial Intelligence & Data Science** at **Matoshri College of Engineering & Research Centre (MCOERC), Nashik**.

- 🌐 **Google Student Ambassador**: Leading tech initiatives at **GDG on Campus**, organizing developer workshops, and mentoring peer developers.
- 🤖 **Full-Stack AI Developer**: Engineering intuitive front-end interfaces backed by high-performance ML pipelines & LLM integrations.
